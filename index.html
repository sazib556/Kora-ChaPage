How a glass gets made

Four steps, about three minutes. We don't skip any of them.

1

Boil the water

Big kettle, charcoal fire, rolling boil.

2

Steep the leaves

Loose leaf goes in until the water turns dark.

3

Add milk and sugar

Thick milk first, then sugar as you like it.

4

Pull and Pour

Pour high between two pots, then into your glass.
